Optional location sharing

You can choose to share your device's current location with Bates for a service-visit record. This is not required to leave a Google review.

You've chosen optional location sharing.

Your phone may now ask for permission to use your location. If you select Allow, the location coordinates and the phone's reported accuracy will be sent automatically to Bates for this QR visit. You can select Don't Allow instead.

Your device may use GPS, Wi-Fi or cell towers; the accuracy can vary.

Starting your one-time location request…

iPhone location permission help
  1. Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services. Make sure Location Services is on, and check Safari Websites if listed.
  2. Open Settings > Apps > Safari > Location and select Ask.
  3. In Safari, open the page menu > Website Settings for this site and set Location to Ask.
  4. If you arrived through the Camera QR scanner, copy this page's full URL (including the visit parameter), open it in the Safari app, and try again.

Your device must approve location access. Bates cannot override a denial; sharing remains optional.

Skip location and go to Google Reviews

You selected Share My Location on the Bates review page. If your phone grants location permission, your one-time coordinates and accuracy will be submitted automatically to Bates. Denying location permission will not send coordinates. You can still leave a Google review without sharing. The page does not continuously track your location. The QR scan may separately record an approximate internet-based location.
