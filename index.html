Optional location sharing

You can choose to share your device's current location with Bates for a service-visit record. This is not required to leave a Google review.

What would be saved? Latitude, longitude, the device's reported accuracy (in meters), and the associated QR scan/technician.

Your device may use GPS, nearby Wi-Fi, or cell towers. Accuracy varies. A location permission prompt will appear only if you tap the button.

iPhone location permission help
  1. Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services. Make sure Location Services is on, and check Safari Websites if listed.
  2. Open Settings > Apps > Safari > Location and select Ask.
  3. In Safari, open the page menu > Website Settings for this site and set Location to Ask.
  4. If you arrived through the Camera QR scanner, copy this page's full URL (including the visit parameter), open it in the Safari app, and try again.

Your device must approve location access. Bates cannot override a denial; sharing remains optional.

Skip Location — Continue to Google Reviews

Only tapping Send Location to Bates transmits coordinates to Bates. The page does not continuously monitor your location. Bates stores the coordinates only if the GPS master switch is enabled. The QR scan itself may separately record an approximate internet-based location.
